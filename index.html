How it works

Job and exam deadlines close on different days, and the real work — a password, a fee, a challan, a document — is due before the last date. Add each deadline, tick off its steps as you finish them, and the tracker shows the live countdown and which step is still blocking you. Your list is saved in your browser, so it is still there when you come back.

Demo example pre-filled below with sample deadlines: four real public closing dates for November–December 2026. Add your own, change any date, or delete the samples — the countdowns update instantly.
